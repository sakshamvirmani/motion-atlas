# Community conduct

Motion Atlas exists to help people learn without embarrassment or gatekeeping.
In project spaces, be patient, specific, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas and observable behavior, not a person's ability or identity.
- Welcome beginner questions and explain unfamiliar terms.
- Give source and license credit accurately.
- Protect private information and avoid posting sensitive security details.
- Accept corrections and make room for people to revise their work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing,
deliberate plagiarism, spam, and sustained bad-faith disruption are not
acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the
community. If a problem involves a security vulnerability, use the private
reporting path described in `SECURITY.md` rather than a public issue.
