# Original-work and licensing policy

Last reviewed: 2026-08-11

This policy is a production gate for Motion Atlas. It is operational guidance,
not legal advice.

## Default rule

Learn from sources; do not reproduce their expression.

Motion Atlas may independently explain facts, ideas, APIs, procedures, mental
models, and observed behavior. It must not copy another creator's prose,
lesson order, exercises, code, illustrations, screenshots, animation assets,
recordings, or distinctive visual composition merely because the material can
be viewed online.

## Allowed source uses

1. **Reference only.** Read a source, verify a claim, write an independent
   explanation, and link to the original. This is the default.
2. **Short quotation.** Use only when the exact wording is necessary, keep it
   brief, mark it as a quotation, identify the author and source, and record the
   reason in the ledger. Do not use quotations as lesson filler.
3. **Licensed reuse.** Reuse only when the exact license permits the planned
   format, modification, distribution, and commercial status. Follow every
   attribution, notice, source-disclosure, and share-alike condition.
4. **Written permission.** Preserve the permission and its scope in the project
   records before publishing the material.
5. **Public domain.** Record why the work is in the public domain and the
   jurisdictional basis. Do not infer public-domain status from age or from a
   missing notice.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Do not treat “free,” “open source,” “educational,” or “on GitHub” as a license.
- Do not copy from a source with no license.
- Do not assume that credit cures an otherwise unauthorized copy.
- Do not depend on a vague percentage or word-count idea of fair use.
- Do not trace or lightly restyle distinctive graphics or course interfaces.
- Do not remove copyright, attribution, or license notices.
- Do not mix ShareAlike content into Motion Atlas unless the compatibility and
  resulting distribution obligations have been reviewed first.
- Do not paste third-party code into examples until its software license and
  notice requirements are recorded.

## Course production checklist

Every lesson must have a ledger entry or source block that records:

- source title, creator/publisher, direct URL, and access date;
- what factual claim or API behavior it supports;
- use type: reference, quotation, licensed reuse, permission, or public domain;
- license name and version when reuse occurs;
- attribution text and change notice when required;
- reviewer and review date; and
- the Motion Atlas files or lesson slugs affected.

If license status is unclear, the content is blocked from publication. Replace
it with an independently created explanation or obtain permission.

## Motion Atlas licensing

- Original software and code samples: MIT License.
- Original educational prose, diagrams, exercises, and non-code visual assets:
  CC BY 4.0 unless marked otherwise.
- Third-party material: remains under its original terms and is excluded from
  the Motion Atlas content license.

The site must link to both the content license and the source ledger before the
open-source repository is announced.
